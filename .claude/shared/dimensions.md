# Dimensions and Ratings

What an Interview rates, on which scale, and how a Rating is proposed.

## Scale

A Rating is Below, Meets or Exceeds the Position's career level, or Not assessed. It is a word, never a number.

## Dimensions

Deliverables use these names and definitions verbatim.

| Block | Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Soft | Communication | Explains ideas clearly, in a structure the listener can follow, for technical and non-technical audiences. |
| Soft | Problem Solving | Breaks an unfamiliar problem down, weighs options and trade-offs, and reaches an approach they can defend. |
| Soft | Collaboration | Works with others toward a shared result, credits the team, and handles disagreement constructively. |
| Soft | Growth Mindset | Learns from mistakes and feedback, is honest about gaps, and keeps skills current. |
| Soft | Ownership | Takes responsibility for outcomes end to end, including follow-through when things go wrong. |
| Soft | English | Understands and expresses technical and day-to-day content in English at the level the Position requires. |
| Technical | Platform knowledge | Depth on the Position's platform and its stack, across the sub-areas from the handbook or the JD. |
| Technical | Architecture & design | Designs solutions that fit the problem, weighing scalability, maintainability and trade-offs. |
| Technical | Code quality & practices | Produces and reviews work to team standards: testing, reviews, tooling, performance. |
| Technical | Debugging & troubleshooting | Finds and fixes problems methodically, including unfamiliar ones, with the right diagnostic tools. |
| Technical | Stack breadth | Works competently beyond their main layer: adjacent frontend, backend, devops and integrations. |
| Leadership | People leadership & mentoring | Grows and guides a team through mentoring, review and feedback, leading rather than doing everything themselves. |
| Leadership | Stakeholder & client management | Manages expectations, scope and difficult conversations with clients and internal stakeholders. |
| Leadership | Delivery ownership | Drives commitments to completion on time and at quality, tracked with KPIs. |

- Every Position gets the 6 soft and the 5 technical Dimensions.
- Leadership roles (Tech Leader L8, Expert, Manager, Director) add the 3 leadership Dimensions and keep the full technical core, measured against their role: for a Director, Meets in Code quality & practices means setting standards, not writing code.
- English is rated against the level the JD sets: B2 minimum, B1 as the exception. The Interview does not assess it; its result reaches the hiring lead separately. This is the one exception to "only interview evidence counts".

## What Meets means

The Dimensions stay fixed; what Meets means changes by track and level.

- The rubric lives in the handbook's `evaluation-criteria/`: soft and leadership in one shared page, the technical core in one page per track. Skills only read it.
- While a rubric page is missing or `Pending`, an individual-contributor track reads `career-paths/<track>.md` and `reference/career-levels.md`, and the artifact says "rubric pending: using career-paths". A leadership role reads `roles/<role>.md`, and the artifact says "rubric pending: using roles".
- Platform knowledge's sub-areas come from `technologies/<platform>.md` (Stack by layer) and from the Position's own Requirements. They are collected as evidence, never rated one by one.
- When the handbook lacks the platform or the role (Sitecore, Software Architect), sub-areas and expectations come only from the JD's Requirements. The skill says the handbook does not cover it and keeps to the stack the JD names.

## Rating rules

Wherever a Rating is proposed:

- Only interview evidence counts, never the CV.
- Not covered in the Interview: Not assessed, never a default value.
- Asked and followed up with no concrete example: Below, because that is evidence.
- A confident wrong answer counts against the Candidate more than an honest gap.
- No usable evidence: Not assessed, never inferred.

## Flags

The lists are prompts, not a checklist: write only what was observed, each flag with its evidence, and add others the Interview showed. The Interview guide draws its "Watch for" signals from them; the Debrief records them.

- **Red:** consistently bashes previous employers · cannot give specific examples · defensive when challenged · resistance to learning · takes all credit without mentioning the team · asks no questions · confident wrong answer.
- **Green:** honest about gaps and proposes how to address them · explains complex concepts clearly · discusses challenges respectfully · asks thoughtful questions about the team · acknowledges mistakes and lessons learned · enthusiastic about the domain · mentions helping others grow.
