# Level equivalence

The only equivalence between Globant P&L seniorities, handbook levels and HR job descriptions: no official one exists.

| Globant P&L | Handbook | HR job description | Years |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid Level | L5 Semi Senior Advanced | Semi Senior II | 4 |
| Sr | L6 Senior Level 1 | Senior I | 5 |
| Sr Level 2 | L7 Senior Level 2 | Senior II | 6 |
| Sr 3, leads people | L8 Senior Level 3 (Tech Leader) | Tech Lead I / II | 7+ / 10+ |
| Sr 3, does not lead people | L7 Senior Level 2 | Experience Senior I / II | 7 / 8 |
| Subject Matter Expert | Expert | Software Architect | 11 |
| Manager | Tech Manager | Technical Manager | 11 |
| Director | Director | DXP Technology Director | 13 |

- For every Sr 3 line, the intake round asks whether the person leads the team. When they do not, `opportunity.md` flags "priced as Sr 3, IC Position mapped to L7".
- When a P&L has "Architect" as the Client's Role Name on a Sr 3 line, the intake round asks whether the Position is an Expert or a Tech Leader.
- Globant's `Lead` has no row: it appears only on practice roles, which are not DXP technical roles and get no JD.
- Years come from the HR job descriptions, not from the Globant P&L band. The two differ only on Sr 3 (8–10 vs 7+), and that difference gets no flag.
- The Debrief's calibrated level uses the Handbook column. Only the hiring lead writes it.
- The HR job description column names a file in `hr-job-descriptions/` (its README maps names to files).
