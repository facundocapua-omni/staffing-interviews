## Screening

1. "Tell me about a project where you led the team's technical delivery. How many developers did you manage, and what did you own?" A good answer names the team size, the decisions they made and the delivery outcome they answered for.
2. "Walk me through a Contentful implementation you led: how did you model the content, and how did the front end consume it?" A good answer covers content types and references, the Content Management or Delivery API, and a decoupled front end.
3. "Have you built a Contentful app with the App Framework? What did it do and where did it run?" A good answer describes an app they built, its location in the editor or as a service, and how it was hosted.
4. "Describe a CMS-to-CMS content migration you led or built." A good answer mentions a scripted extract, transform and load, validation of the migrated content, and rehearsal or delta runs.
5. Check in the CV or profile: 7+ years of software development, at least one role as Tech Lead with direct reports, and hands-on backend work.
6. Run the screening call partly in English: the candidate holds a technical discussion at B2 or higher.

## Not a fit if

- Fewer than 7 years of software development experience
- Has never led a team's technical delivery or managed Developers
- No longer writes code: leads only as a manager
- Has never delivered a Contentful implementation
- Has never built a Contentful app
- Has never led or built a content migration between CMSs
- Cannot hold a client conversation in English at B2
