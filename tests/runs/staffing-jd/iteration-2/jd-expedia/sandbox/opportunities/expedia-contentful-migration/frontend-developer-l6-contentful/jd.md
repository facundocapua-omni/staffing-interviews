# Frontend Developer · Senior Level 1 (L6) · Contentful

**Location:** LATAM; remote

A senior frontend developer who shapes and builds the Next.js front end, fed by Contentful, that will serve a US-based global travel company's partner-facing websites after their migration from AEM.

- [ ] 5 years of frontend development experience `[HR job description]`

## Screening

1. "Tell me about a Next.js front end whose architecture you defined. How did you decide what to render statically, what on the server, and how content stayed fresh?" A good answer covers the App Router, ISR with on-demand revalidation, SSR for dynamic routes and a performance budget.
2. "How did your front end consume a headless CMS, and how did authors preview drafts?" A good answer names Contentful or another headless CMS, the delivery and preview APIs, and draft or preview mode.
3. "How do you test the UI?" A good answer mentions component tests and end-to-end tests with Playwright or an equivalent.
4. Check in the CV or profile: 5 years of frontend development, recent React/Next.js work against a headless CMS, and accessible, responsive UI.
5. Run part of the call in English: the candidate explains a technical choice at B2 or higher.

## Not a fit if

- Fewer than 5 years of frontend development experience
- Has never owned a project's frontend architecture decisions
- No production React and Next.js experience
- Has never worked with Next.js rendering modes (ISR, SSR, preview)
- Has built only server-rendered templates inside a coupled CMS, never a decoupled front end
- No experience integrating REST or GraphQL APIs
- Writes no frontend tests
- English below B2
