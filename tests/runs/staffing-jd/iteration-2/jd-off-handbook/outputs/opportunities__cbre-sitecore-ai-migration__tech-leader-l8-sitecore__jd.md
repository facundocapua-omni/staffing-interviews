# Tech Leader · Senior Level 3 (L8) · Sitecore

**Location:** To be confirmed

A Tech Leader who joins, already in flight, the migration of a global commercial real estate services company from on-premises Sitecore to Sitecore XM Cloud with Next.js, and leads the team's technical delivery alongside the client's own strong technology team.

- [ ] 7+ years of experience in software development `[HR job description]`

- [ ] Experience with Sitecore XM Cloud `[hiring lead]`
- [ ] Experience with Sitecore Headless (JSS / Headless SXA) and Next.js `[hiring lead]`

- [ ] Code review and mentoring of the Developers on the team `[handbook role]` `[handbook level]`

- [ ] English B2 or higher `[suite rule]`

## Screening

1. "Tell me about the last Sitecore XM Cloud project you worked on with a headless front end. What was your role, and what did you design?" A good answer mentions XM Cloud, JSS or Headless SXA with Next.js, hands-on work on the solution, and decisions the candidate made.
2. "Tell me about a time you joined a project already in progress as Tech Lead. What did you do in your first weeks?" A good answer mentions how they learned the codebase and the delivery state, took ownership of delivery, and earned the team's trust.
3. "Describe a technical disagreement with a client's own engineering team. How did you handle it?" A good answer mentions holding a position with technical arguments, laying out options, reaching an agreement and keeping the relationship working.
4. "How do you run code review and mentoring for the Developers on your team, and how do you handle scope changes the client asks for?" A good answer mentions a regular review practice, concrete mentoring, and negotiating scope with the client explicitly.
5. Check in the CV or profile: 7+ years in software development, at least one Tech Lead role managing Developers, and Sitecore XM Cloud and headless (JSS / Headless SXA, Next.js) projects.
6. Check in the CV or profile: on-premises Sitecore projects, which are a plus.
7. Check English at B2 or higher with recruiting's usual test.

## Not a fit if

- Has fewer than 7 years of experience in software development.
- Has worked as a senior individual contributor but never led a team's technical delivery.
- Has only led projects from their start, never taken over one already in flight.
- Has only led delivery where the client had no technology team of its own to answer to.
- Has no hands-on experience with Sitecore XM Cloud.
- Has Sitecore experience without headless (JSS / Headless SXA) and Next.js.
- Is no longer hands-on: cannot design the solution or work in the code.
- Has never reviewed code or mentored Developers.
- Has never dealt directly with a client on communication and scope.
- English below B2.
