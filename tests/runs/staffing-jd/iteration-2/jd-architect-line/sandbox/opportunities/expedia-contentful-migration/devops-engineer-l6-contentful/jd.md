## Screening

1. "Describe the pipeline and environment strategy you designed on your last project." A good answer mentions the environments, the CI/CD stages and quality gates, and why they chose them.
2. "Which AWS services did you set up as code, and with which tool?" A good answer mentions IAM, networking, Lambda or S3, and Terraform, CDK or SST.
3. "How did you configure a CDN to route traffic or roll back a release?" A good answer mentions routing rules, redirects, cache purge, and a rollback that does not need a redeploy.
4. "What did you monitor, and how did alerts reach the team?" A good answer mentions Datadog or an equivalent, dashboards and alerting.
5. Check in the CV or profile: 5+ years of experience, AWS and GitHub-based pipelines.
6. Check: English at B2 or higher, in the screening call.

- Has never designed a CI/CD pipeline.

- Has never written infrastructure as code.
- Has never set up observability with Datadog or an equivalent.
- Has never worked with GitHub.
- Has never configured a CDN's caching, routing or redirects.
- Has only operated existing pipelines, never owned a project's delivery architecture decisions.
