# Tech Director · Director · DXP platforms

## Screening

1. "How many people did you lead in your last role, and how many of them were managers or tech leads?" A good answer mentions managing other leaders, not only individual contributors, and coaching them.
2. "Tell me about a technology strategy you defined: what business goal it served and how you rolled it out." A good answer mentions the business objective, the decisions taken and how adoption was measured.
3. "Have you reorganised a technical team? What did you change in roles or ways of working, and why?" A good answer mentions a concrete structural change and its outcome.
4. "On which DXP or commerce platform did you lead delivery or architecture, and what was your role?" A good answer names at least one of AEM, Contentful, Optimizely or Adobe Commerce, with a leadership role on it.
5. "When did you last review an architecture or a pull request, and what did you push back on?" A good answer mentions recent, concrete review work: scalability, patterns, tests.
6. Check in the CV or profile: hands-on background in at least one language or framework; experience with at least one cloud and DevOps tooling, software security, databases, observability and agile methodologies.
7. Check in the CV or profile: a degree in a related field or equivalent experience, plus complementary studies in architecture, cloud, DevOps or project management.
8. "Tell me about a technical change you introduced against resistance, and how you decided it was worth it." A good answer mentions the data behind the decision and how the change was managed.
9. Check English: it should be B2 or above.

## Not a fit if

- Has only led individual contributors, never other managers or leads
- Has never defined or executed a technology strategy
- Has never designed or changed a technical organisation's structure or ways of working
- Has never worked with sales, delivery, product or executive stakeholders on technical decisions
- Has not led delivery or architecture on any of AEM, Contentful, Optimizely or Adobe Commerce
- Has no recent hands-on architecture or code review work
- Has no backend or frontend development background
- Has no cloud or DevOps experience on any cloud
- Has no experience with software security practices or with database management
- Has no experience with observability tools or agile methodologies
- Has neither a related degree nor equivalent relevant experience, or has no complementary studies in architecture, cloud, DevOps or project management
- Shows no data-driven decision making, change management or problem solving in their track record
- English below B2
