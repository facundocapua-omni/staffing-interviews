## Inconsistencies found

1. Years of experience: the supplied JD asks for 8; `level-equivalence.md` and the HR job description `dxp-technology-director.md` ask for 13. Open (question 3).
2. The supplied JD is written for a hands-on, Adobe Commerce-centred role (writing code, Pull Requests, tracking team hours, Adobe Commerce best practices and core flows, payment gateways and PCI). The handbook's Tech Director sits on the strategic layer and covers the whole unit. Resolved by answers 6, 7 and 8.
3. Reporting line: the supplied JD has the role reporting to the DPX Business Unit Director (indirectly the CEO); the handbook's Tech Director reports to the Global Tech Director. The JD states no reporting line, so the handbook's references to the Global Tech Director are left out of its responsibilities.
4. Optimizely: the hiring lead includes it among the unit's platforms; the handbook's `technologies/optimizely-cloud.md` is a hidden draft ("Optimizely isn't relevant to the team at the moment"). Kept, per answer 7.
5. The supplied JD requires a certification from the DXP unit's training plan, which an external Candidate cannot hold before joining. Resolved by answer 9.
