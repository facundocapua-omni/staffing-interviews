# Omni.Pro: DXP Technology Director

## Context

- **Client:** Omni.Pro, internal: the company staffing a role of its own, for its Digital Experience Platforms (DXP) business unit.
- **Project:** hire the DXP unit's Technology Director.
- **Workstreams:** none; a single leadership Position for the whole unit.
- **Dates:** no Proposal or P&L; start open.
- **Stack signals:** the unit's platforms, as the hiring lead set them: AEM Traditional, AEM Edge Delivery Services, Contentful, Optimizely, Adobe Commerce. The supplied JD's generic engineering stack: backend/frontend languages, cloud and DevOps, security, databases, agile methods, observability.

## Inconsistencies found

1. Layer: the handbook places the Tech Director in the strategic layer (`roles/principles.md`: a Director approving a project's technical decisions is a warning sign), while the supplied JD's responsibilities include operational work (writing code, building modules, tracking the team's hours, handling Pull Requests). Resolved by answer 4.
2. Reports: the handbook's Tech Director line-manages the Tech Managers and the Expert; the supplied JD has the Position leading "the development team" directly. Open: see answer 9.
3. Platform: the supplied JD is written for Adobe Commerce only (e-commerce mission, Adobe Commerce practices, payment gateways, PCI); the unit spans CMS and Commerce platforms. Resolved by answers 5 and 8.
4. Optimizely: the hiring lead includes it among the unit's platforms, while the handbook's `technologies/optimizely-cloud.md` is a draft marked "Optimizely isn't relevant to the team at the moment". Kept as the hiring lead set it.
5. Client name: the supplied JD names the company only as "LA EMPRESA"; the slug `omnipro-dxp-technology-director` was proposed in the intake round and not answered (answer 10).

## Intake answers

1. Is the client internal? **Answer:** yes, the company's own DXP business unit (stated in the request).
2. Who supplied the job description? **Answer:** the hiring lead.
3. Which handbook role: Global Tech Director or Tech Director? **Answer:** Tech Director (`regional-director`), at the Director level. Years from `level-equivalence.md` (Director: 13), which match the supplied JD.
4. The supplied JD's operational responsibilities against the handbook's strategic layer: what enters the JD? **Answer:** a mix: strategic and management, with hands-on technical judgment (reviewing architecture and code). Writing code as a task and tracking the team's hours stay out.
5. Which platforms? **Answer:** the whole DXP unit: AEM Traditional, AEM EDS, Contentful, Optimizely, Adobe Commerce. The supplied JD's Adobe Commerce-specific items stay out.
6. The supplied JD's technical list: all, at least one per group, or Nice to have? **Answer:** each group is a Must have, with its technologies as examples ("at least one cloud: AWS, GCP or Azure").
7. The DXP training plan certification: Must have at hiring or obligation after joining? **Answer:** an obligation once the person joins; it stays out of the JD.
8. Each platform: Must have or Nice to have? **Answer:** at least one of the unit's platforms is a Must have (having led delivery or architecture on it); the others are Nice to have.
9. Who reports to the Position: the Tech Managers and the Expert (handbook) or the development team directly (supplied JD)? **Open:** no answer yet. Waiting on it: the handbook's "Coaching senior managers" Must have, and the line-management and delivery KPI review responsibilities.
10. Opportunity slug `omnipro-dxp-technology-director`? **Open:** no answer yet; used as proposed. Waiting on it: the folder name only.
11. Headcount, start, country and remote mode? **Open:** no answer yet. Waiting on it: the headcount and start in the table above, and the JD's Logistics (country, start, full-time).
