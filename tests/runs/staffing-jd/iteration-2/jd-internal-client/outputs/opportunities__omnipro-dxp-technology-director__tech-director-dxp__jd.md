# Tech Director · Director · DXP (AEM, Contentful, Optimizely, Adobe Commerce)

**Location:** To be confirmed; remote, with on-site presence at a branch when needed

The technology director of our own Digital Experience Platforms (DXP) business unit: sets and runs the unit's technical strategy, leads its technical teams, and keeps hands-on judgment over the architecture and code they deliver on AEM, Contentful, Optimizely and Adobe Commerce.

## Screening

1. "How many years have you worked in software engineering, and which of them in technology leadership?" A good answer adds up to 13 or more.
2. "Tell me about a unit or region whose technical strategy you owned: what standards and governance did you set, and how did you organise its teams?" A good answer mentions several teams, a strategy they defined and executed, and standards or a quality bar applied across them.
3. "How have you grown the engineers and leads on your teams?" A good answer mentions mentoring, feedback, and people they developed into larger roles.
4. "Give an example of aligning priorities between engineering and business or executive stakeholders." A good answer mentions a negotiation with sales, delivery or leadership and how the technical topic was explained.
5. "On which of AEM, Contentful, Optimizely or Adobe Commerce have you led delivery or architecture, and in what role?" A good answer names at least one, with a project where they led its delivery or architecture; more than one is a plus.
6. "What was the last architecture or code you reviewed, and what did you change?" A good answer mentions scalability, microservices or integrations, and code standards, patterns or tests.
7. Check in the CV or profile: hands-on background in at least one of Java, Python, Node.js, .NET, React or Angular; at least one cloud (AWS, GCP or Azure) with Kubernetes or Terraform; security, databases, agile methods and observability tooling.
8. Check in the CV or profile: a degree in a related field or equivalent experience, plus further studies in architecture, cloud, DevOps or technology project management.
9. Check in the CV or profile: certifications such as AWS Solutions Architect, Google Cloud Architect, TOGAF or PMP, and experience with ISO/IEC 27001, FinOps or AI in development processes.
10. English: B2, checked with recruiting's English assessment.

## Not a fit if

- Fewer than 13 years of experience.
- Has led a single project or team only, never a unit's or region's technical strategy, governance and org design.
- Has never managed or developed engineering teams.
- Cannot show communicating and aligning priorities with executive and business stakeholders.
- Has never led delivery or architecture on AEM, Contentful, Optimizely or Adobe Commerce.
- Cannot design or review scalable architectures and code.
- No hands-on background in any of Java, Python, Node.js, .NET, React or Angular.
- No experience with any of AWS, GCP or Azure, or with DevOps tooling.
- No grounding in software security, or in SQL, NoSQL or Big Data databases.
- Has never worked in Scrum, SAFe or Kanban, or with monitoring and observability tools.
- Neither a related degree nor equivalent experience, or no further studies in architecture, cloud, DevOps or technology project management.
- Cannot show data-driven decisions, change management or cross-area collaboration.
- English below B2.
