## Screening

1. "How did you decide between static, regenerated and server-rendered pages in a Next.js project, and how did content updates reach the cache?" A good answer mentions ISR or on-demand revalidation (revalidateTag/revalidatePath) triggered by a CMS webhook.
2. "How did authors preview unpublished content on a headless site you built?" A good answer mentions Draft Mode or a preview API, and click-to-edit or live preview.
3. "What did you check to keep SEO intact on a multilingual site?" A good answer mentions hreflang, canonicals, redirects and sitemaps.
4. "Which frontend architecture decisions did you own, and what performance budget did you hold?" A good answer names concrete decisions and metrics (Core Web Vitals, bundle size).
5. Check in the CV or profile: about 5 years of frontend work, Next.js in production, at least one headless CMS.
6. Check English at B2 or higher.

## Not a fit if

- Has never owned frontend architecture decisions on a project.
