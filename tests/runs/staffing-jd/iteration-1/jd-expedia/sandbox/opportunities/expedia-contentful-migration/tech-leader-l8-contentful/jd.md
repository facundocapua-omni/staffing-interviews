## Screening

1. "Tell me about a team you led technically: how many developers, what you owned, and how you sequenced the delivery." A good answer mentions managing developers day to day, code review and mentoring, and putting the riskiest work first.
2. "How would you model pages built from reusable sections and blocks in Contentful, across several locales?" A good answer mentions content types and references, locales and fallback, and using environments to change the model safely.
3. "Have you built a Contentful app? What did it do and where did it run?" A good answer mentions the App Framework, editor locations or a backend function, and the Content Management API.
4. "How would you migrate tens of thousands of entries from another CMS into Contentful?" A good answer mentions a scripted extract–transform–load, idempotent loads by a stable ID, validation runs and rehearsals.
5. Check in the CV or profile: at least 7 years of development experience, a Tech Lead role with direct responsibility for developers, and a headless or Contentful project.
6. Check English at B2 or higher in a conversation with the client in mind.

## Not a fit if

- Has never led developers on a project: only individual-contributor or architecture roles.
- Has no hands-on Contentful or comparable headless CMS work: content modeling and the management/delivery APIs.
- Has never built a content migration or a content-serving API.
- English below B2.
