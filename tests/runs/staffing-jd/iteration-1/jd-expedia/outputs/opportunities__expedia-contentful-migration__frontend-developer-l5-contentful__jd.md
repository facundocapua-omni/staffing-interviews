1. "Walk me through a set of related pages you built end to end in Next.js: where the data came from and how you handled loading and errors." A good answer mentions fetching from a CMS or API, shared components, and error and loading states.
2. "When would you render a page statically, regenerate it, or render it on the server?" A good answer distinguishes static generation, ISR and SSR in the App Router.
3. "How do you test your components?" A good answer mentions component tests and Playwright or an equivalent end-to-end tool.
4. Check in the CV or profile: about 4 years of frontend work, React and Next.js, ideally with a headless CMS.
5. Check English at B2 or higher.
