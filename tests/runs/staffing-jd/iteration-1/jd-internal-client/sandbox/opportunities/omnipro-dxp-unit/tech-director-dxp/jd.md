## Screening

1. "How many people have you led, and how many of them were managers or tech leads themselves?" A good answer mentions leading managers of teams (not only individual contributors), and shaping team structure and roles.
2. "Tell me about a technical strategy you defined and rolled out across several teams or accounts." A good answer mentions the strategy, how standards were adopted, and how consistency and quality were tracked.
3. "When did you last review an architecture or a pull request, and what did you change?" A good answer mentions a concrete, recent review with a technical reason, not only delegation.
4. "Which of AEM, Edge Delivery Services, Contentful, Optimizely and Adobe Commerce have you led delivery on, and in what role?" A good answer names projects on these platforms and the architecture decisions taken on them.
5. "Which clouds, observability tools and security frameworks have your teams worked with?" A good answer names at least one cloud (AWS, GCP or Azure), one observability tool and one security practice (ISO 27001, OWASP or Zero Trust).
6. Check in the CV or profile: 13+ years in software, with recent years in a director-level or multi-team engineering management role.
7. Check English at B2 or higher.

## Not a fit if

- Has led only a single team of individual contributors, never managers or tech leads.
- Has moved fully away from technical work and cannot review architecture or code.
- Has no delivery experience on any of the DXP unit's platforms (AEM, Edge Delivery Services, Contentful, Optimizely, Adobe Commerce).
- Has fewer than 13 years of experience.
- English below B2.
