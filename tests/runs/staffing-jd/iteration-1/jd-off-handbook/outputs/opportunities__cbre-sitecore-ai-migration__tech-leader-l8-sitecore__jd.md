## Screening

1. "Tell me about the last team you led: how many people, what you were accountable for, and how you handled someone who was struggling." A good answer names a team they managed, not only advised, and a concrete people situation they handled.
2. "Have you taken over technical leadership of a project that was already running? What did you do in your first weeks?" A good answer describes getting to know an existing codebase, decisions and team, and how they set direction without restarting the work.
3. "Describe a disagreement with a client's own technical team. How did you hold or change your position?" A good answer shows a technical argument backed by evidence, respect for the client's expertise, and a resolution.
4. "Which Sitecore XM Cloud projects have you worked on, and how was the head built?" A good answer mentions JSS or Headless SXA with Next.js, and their own hands-on part in it.
5. Check in the CV or profile: 7+ years of software development, at least one role leading a development team, and Sitecore XM Cloud with Next.js named on a specific project.
6. Check English at B2 or higher.

## Not a fit if

- Has led only technically (reviews, direction) without managing the people on a team.
- Has no hands-on Sitecore XM Cloud experience, or knows Sitecore only through MVC without the headless (JSS / Headless SXA) and Next.js stack.
- Has fewer than 7 years of software development experience.
- Has only ever started projects from scratch, never taken over one mid-delivery.
- English below B2.
