# Tech Director · Director · DXP (AEM Traditional, AEM EDS, Contentful, Optimizely Cloud, Adobe Commerce)

**Location:** remote; on site at the relevant branch when needed

## Screening

1. "How many managers or team leads report to you today, and how did you design that structure?" A good answer mentions managing managers (not only developers), org design decisions, and coaching senior people.
2. "Tell me about a technology strategy you defined and rolled out across several teams or accounts." A good answer mentions the standards or practices adopted, how adoption was driven, and how quality was kept consistent.
3. "Which of AEM, Edge Delivery Services, Contentful, Optimizely and Adobe Commerce have you worked with, and at what level?" A good answer shows architecture-level knowledge of the platforms and recent hands-on review of architecture and code.
4. Check in the CV or profile: experience with at least one cloud (AWS, GCP or Azure), a backend and a frontend stack, security practices (OWASP, ISO 27001, Zero Trust), agile methodologies and observability tooling.
5. Check in the CV or profile: a degree in a related field or equivalent experience, and English B2 or above.

## Not a fit if

- Has never managed managers or senior technical leads
- Has a purely delivery or project-management profile, without hands-on review of architecture or code
- Has no architecture-level experience with the DXP platforms listed
- Has no cloud experience (AWS, GCP or Azure)
- English below B2
