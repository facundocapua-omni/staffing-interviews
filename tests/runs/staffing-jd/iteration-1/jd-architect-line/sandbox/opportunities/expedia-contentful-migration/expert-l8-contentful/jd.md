## Screening

1. "Tell me about a Contentful architecture you designed: the content model, the locales and how the front end consumed it." A good answer mentions content types and references, locale fallback, environments for model changes, and the Delivery or GraphQL API.
2. "Have you built a Contentful app, or a service that serves CMS content to Salesforce Marketing Cloud?" A good answer mentions the App Framework or app functions, and how content reaches SFMC at send time.
3. "How would you migrate about 100,000 content items out of a legacy CMS without a big-bang cutover?" A good answer mentions a scripted idempotent pipeline, stable IDs, validation on every run, and phased traffic ramps with rollback.
4. Check in the CV or profile: 11+ years in software, with architecture roles that span backend, front end and cloud, and a role that set standards for teams beyond their own.

## Not a fit if

- Has led teams but never owned a solution's architecture
- Has worked with Contentful only as an author, or has no headless CMS architecture experience
- Has no integration work with Salesforce Marketing Cloud or a similar marketing platform, and no Contentful app development
- Has fewer than 11 years of software experience
- English below B2
