1. "How does a page in your Next.js app get updated when an author publishes in the CMS?" A good answer mentions ISR or cache tags, on-demand revalidation from a webhook, and how preview reads drafts.

3. "How do you handle several locales for SEO?" A good answer mentions locale routing, hreflang, canonicals and redirects.
4. Check in the CV or profile: 5+ years, production Next.js with a headless CMS (Contentful preferred), and work on a shared component library.
